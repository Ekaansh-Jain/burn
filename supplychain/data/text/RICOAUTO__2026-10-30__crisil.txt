Detailed Rationale
Crisil Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of Rico Auto Industries Limited (RAIL; a part of the Rico group) to
‘Negative’
from ‘Stable’ while reaffirming the rating at ‘
Crisil A
’. Crisil Ratings has also reaffirmed its ‘Crisil A1’
rating on the short-te
rm bank facilities of the company.
The revision in outlook factors in the moderation
in business risk profile marked by
lower
operating profitability
and thus lower cash accruals
. Crisil Ratings believes that the business risk profile, particularly operating profitability, shall remain subdued due to
higher logistic cost
and a lagged pass-through of input inflation. While the management is negotiating to recover such amounts and has started to pass on such costs on a monthly basis from July 2026, operating
profitability shall remain 8-8.5%. Consequently, net cash accruals shall remain lower at Rs 100-150 crores. Going forward, any delay in
recovery
or further moderation in margins shall impact cash flows, which remains a key rating sensitivity factor.
The ratings continue to reflect the established position of Rico group supported by diversified customer profile and a key supplier of critical components like braking system, aluminum engine blocks and transmission part. The group is expected to achieve reve
nue growth of
12-15
% in current fiscal and next as compared to Rs.2480 crore achieved in fiscal 2026. The growth will be driven by increased demand from existing customers and addition of new customers. The operating profitability is expected to remain 8-8.5% over the medium term as compared to 9.33% seen in previous fiscal.
The financial risk profile shall continue to support the rating, driven by healthy leverage
of
less than 2 times
and interest coverage
of more than 4 times
.
The group has no major inc
remental capex plans over the medium term and thus will maintain healthy financial risk profile.
The ratings continue to reflect the established market position, diversified clientele in the automotive (auto) components industry and healthy financial risk profile of the group. These strengths are partially offset by modest operating efficiency and
large working capital requirements
.
Analytical Approach
Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of RAIL and its subsidiaries. This
is because all the entities, collectively referred to as the Rico group, have common management and business and financial linkages.

Key Rating Drivers - Weaknesses
Moderated operating
margins
Crisil Ratings believes that the business risk profile, particularly operating profitability, shall remain subdued due to one-time air freight fo
r scheduling mismatches and a lagged pass-through of input inflation. While the management is negotiating to recover such amounts and has started to pass on such costs on a monthly basis from July 2026, operating profitability shall remain 8-8.5% lower than the previously expected 10-11% for fiscal 2027 (
at 4.2% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027
;
9.33% in fiscal 2026)
. Around
~75% of the revenue is earned from OEMs with monthly price pass-through mechanism to adjust for
these
costs
and
recoveries of Rs 70
–90 crore is expected in fiscal 2027 itself. Sustenance of operating income and margin
s
leading to better-than-expected net cash accrual
s
,
shall remain a key
monitorable.
Large working capital requirement:
The group’s gross current assets stood at 110-150 days over the past few fiscals; 102 days as of March 31, 2026, primarily due to receivables of 50-60 days, resulting from extended credit to customers. The working capital cycle is stretched due to transit times and buffer stock maintained in customer war
ehouses, leading to a prolonged receivable cycle. This has resulted in significant dependence on bank limits, with 81% utilization over the last 12 months ended August 2026. As the group scales up, working capital requirements will increase, and the group’s ability to efficiently manage the same, thereby reducing the reliance on external debt will remain a key monitorable
.
Liquidity
Strong
Bank limit utilisation was around 81% for the 12 months through August 2026. Expected cash accrual of Rs 150–200 cror
e should suffice to cover the yearly debt obligation of Rs 110–130 crore over the medium term. Current ratio stood at 0.87 time as on March 31, 2026. The promoters are likely to extend need-based funds (equity and unsecured loans) to aid operations.

About the
Group
RAIL, a part of the Ludhiana-based Rico group, was incorporated in March 1983. The group manufactures machined engine and die-casting and clutch parts for leading two- and four-wheeler OEMs across the globe. The company is listed on the National Stock Exchange and Bombay Stock Exchange.
AAN Engineering Industries Limited, established in January 2010, is a wholly owned subsidiary of RAIL. The company manufactures and assembles mechanical fuses and metal parts for electronic fuses, mainly for aerospace and
defence industries.
Rico Auto Industries Inc, USA, wholly owned subsidiary of RAIL, trades in auto components and provides warehousing and logistics support to OEMs and Tier I customers in the US.
Rico Auto Industries (UK) Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of RAIL, trades in auto components and provides warehousing and logistics support to OEMs and Tier I customers in North America and Brazil. It has been non-operational from the past two years.
Rico Friction Technologies Private Limited is 70% hel
d by RAIL. The company manufactures friction materials for clutches and other applications.
Rico Jinfei Wheels Limited (96.31% held by RAIL) manufactures aluminum alloy wheels for two-wheelers. Rico Fluidtronics Limited is already merged with Rico Jinfei Wheels Limited.
The group has 16 manufacturing facilities in Haryana, Uttarakhand, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan and Gujarat. It is headed by Arvind Kapur (Chairman, CEO & Managing Director).
