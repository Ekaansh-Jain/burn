Detailed rationale
Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its ‘Crisil AA-/Positive/Crisil A1+’ ratings on the bank facilities of Sharda Motor Industries
Ltd (SMIL).
The continuation of positive outlook reflects expectation of Crisil Ratings that the company will maintain its robust business
risk profile, driven by its strong market position in exhaust and suspension systems for passenger cars, utility vehicles, light
commercial vehicles, medium and heavy commercial vehicles and vans along with pan India presence and sustained
healthy profitability. Revenue remained stable at Rs 2,837 crore in fiscal 2025 as against Rs 2810 crore in fiscal 2024. This
was due to the decrease in market share with their exhaust customers because of product diversification strides being made
by SMIL with suspension and global verticals targeting different geographies. However, the operating margin has improved
to over 14% owing to the cost efficiency measures undertaken by the company. This is subjected to the company’s proactive
thinking and investment in research and development (R&D) for auto components which in turn gives SMIL a first mover’s
advantage in the market, hence improving the operating margin. Sustained improvement in revenue along with stable
operating margin of over 12% will remain monitorable.
The company was able to maintain strong capital structure with networth of Rs 1214.51 crore as on March 31, 2025, on the
back of steady accretion to reserve, which is expected to improve further. The total outside liabilities to tangible networth
(TOLTNW) ratio is maintained below 1 time during the three fiscals ended March 31, 2025. It does not have any long-term
debt; thus, the leverage position is expected to remain comfortable and in similar range over the medium term.
The ratings continue to reflect SMIL’s established market position, healthy relationships with customers, efficient working
capital management and strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by risks of customer concentration
in revenue, susceptibility to increase in raw material prices and pricing pressure from original equipment manufacturers
(OEMs).
Analytical approach
Crisil Ratings has evaluated the standalone business and financial risk profiles of SMIL.
Key rating drivers and detailed description
Strengths:
Established market position and strong customer base: Experience of around three decades in manufacturing
exhaust systems has enabled the promoter to develop heathy relationships with major OEMs for which SMIL is the
preferred supplier of independent front suspension systems. Apart from exhaust systems, SMIL manufactures and
supplies various suspension systems and trades in catalytic convertors for its customers. SMIL has ~30% value market
share in India for the passenger vehicle and light commercial vehicle segments and has been catering to both domestic
and international markets. The company has strong in-house R&D capabilities for design and testing for exhaust
systems.
Efficient working capital management: SMIL had high unencumbered cash balance of more than Rs 700 crore as
on March 31, 2025 and nil utilisation of fund-based limit for the 12 months through January 2025. Receivables and
inventory have been at 40-50 days each in the three fiscals ended March 31, 2025. The working capital cycle

is partially supported by the company’s ability to stretch its payables, which have been at 80-100 days in the two fiscals
ended March 31, 2025, and are estimated to have remained at similar level over the medium term.
Strong financial risk profile: The company had strong capital structure as reflected in low TOLTNW ratio of 0.53 time
as on March 31, 2025. Strong capital structure is supported by healthy networth of Rs 1214.51 crore as on March 31,
2025, on the back of steady accretion to reserve and low debt. Networth is expected to improve further, supported by
stable operating margin over the medium term. With regular maintenance capital expenditure (capex) of Rs 70-100
crore and in the absence of any major debt-funded capex over the medium term, the capital structure is expected to
remain strong. Debt protection metrics remained robust with interest coverage ratio of 73.54 times and strong net cash
accrual to adjusted debt ratio in fiscal 2025, which are expected to be in similar range in fiscal 2026.
Weaknesses:
Customer concentration risks in revenue: SMIL only has exhausts and suspension line businesses, along with
trading of catalytic convertors for various OEMs. The top two customers in the exhaust business accounted for ~75% of
total revenue in fiscal 2025. Revenue concentration risk is expected to remain high because of reduced product portfolio
with business arrangements already made between various OEMs and their vendors for BS-VI products. SMIL has
expertise in exhaust systems since inception, which is its core segment and will continue to contribute a significant
portion to overall revenue. Contribution from exhaust systems was more than 85% to the total revenue in fiscal 2025.
Decline in customer concentration, over the medium term, will be monitorable and critical factor for ratings. However, to
reduce the dependence over the exhaust system for passenger vehicles, the company is increasing its presence
supplying the exhaust systems to genset manufacturers in the overseas market.
Susceptibility to increase in raw material prices and pricing pressure from OEMs: The company has limited
bargaining power with OEMs, which periodically revise prices based on their financial standing and willingness. As such
benefit in operating margin comes with a lag. SMIL has strong market position and niche product profile which enables
it to pass on price increases, although with a lag, supported by healthy and sustained earnings before interest, taxes,
depreciation and amortisation margin.
Liquidity: Superior
Annual cash accrual is expected at Rs 250-300 crore against nil term debt obligation over the medium term and will cushion
liquidity. The current ratio was healthy at 2.03 times as on March 31, 2025. Cash and bank balance was high or moderate
around Rs 198.9 crore as on March 31, 2025. Liquid investment was around Rs 707 core in shares, debentures and mutual
funds as on March 31, 2025. Low gearing and moderate networth support financial flexibility and provide cushion in case of
any adverse conditions or downturn in the business.
Outlook: Positive
SMIL’s healthy growth in revenue and sustained operating margin will strengthen the credit risk profile over the medium
term.
