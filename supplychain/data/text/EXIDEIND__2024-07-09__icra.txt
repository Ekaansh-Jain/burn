About the company
Exide Industries Limited is a leading lead-acid battery manufacturer in India. The company started operations as Associated
Battery Makers (Eastern) Ltd., a subsidiary of Chloride Overseas UK, and was acquired by the Rajan Raheja Group in 1993. In
1995, the company was renamed as Exide Industries Limited. In 1998, EIL took over the battery business of Standard Batteries
Limited (SBL), the then second largest battery manufacturer in India, along with four of its factories and the Standard Furukawa
brand. As on date, EIL has one of the largest storage-battery manufacturing capacities in India with regionally diversified
manufacturing facilities. The company is setting up a 6-GWh Li-ion cell manufacturing facility at Bengaluru in the first phase,
through its subsidiary, Exide Energy Solutions Limited (EESL), and plans to expand to 12-GWh based on demand, once the first
phase is completed. EIL is already present in Li-ion battery pack assembly.
