Detailed Rationale
Crisil Ratings has reaffirmed its
‘
Crisil AA/Stable/Crisil A1+
’ ratings on the bank
facilities
Suprajit Engineering Limited
(SEL; part of the Suprajit group
).
The ratings continue to reflect the group’s established market position in the auto component industry, diversified revenue profile, and strong operating efficiency alon
g with strong financial risk profile. These strengths are partially offset by susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and cyclicality in end-user industries.
Analytical Approach
Crisil Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of SEL and its wholly owned subsidiaries and step-down subsidiaries as there exists significant operational and financial fungibilities between the entities.

Key Rating Drivers - Strengths
Established market position in India, diversified revenue profile and strong operating efficiency:
The
group maintains strong leadership position in its core product segments, backed by extensive manufacturing expertise, technological capabilities and established relationships with original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). The group is the largest automotive cable manufacturer in India and the world's largest supp
lier of two-wheeler control cables, providing a strong competitive advantage across domestic and international markets. The group expanded into non-automotive segments through strategic acquisitions, including the addition of lighting business, while also augmenting capacities in its cable business.
The operating income grew by 16.72% to Rs 3,825 crore in fiscal 2026 from Rs 3,277 crore in fiscal 2025, driven by broad-based growth across key business verticals. The operating margin improved to 10.97% in f
iscal 2026 from 10.74% in fiscal 2025, supported by supply-chain optimisation, restructuring initiatives, improved performance of acquired businesses and high capacity utilisation. Over the medium term, revenue growth and profitability are expected to benefit from economies of scale, enhanced operational efficiency, and the group's market leadership, supporting the pass-through of increase in key raw material costs to customers.
Strong financial risk profile:
The group's financial risk profile remained st
rong, supported by networth of Rs 1,171 crore as on March 31, 2026, resulting in gearing of 0.67 time and total outside liabilities to tangible networth ratio of 1.40 times. Debt protection metrics also remained comfortable with interest coverage and net cash accrual to adjusted debt ratios of 6.60 times and 0.37 time, respectively, in fiscal 2026. In the absence of any significant debt-funded capital expenditure (capex) or acquisitions in the pipeline, the group's financial risk profile is expected to rema
in strong over the medium term.
Key Rating Drivers - We
a
knesses
Susceptibility to volatility in raw material prices and competitive pressure:
The group's profitability remains exposed to fluctuation in key raw material prices and intense competition in the automotive component industry. While the group has demonstrated the ability to partially pass on increases in input costs to customers through price negotiations and operational efficiency, any sharp movement in commodity prices or sustained pricing pre
ssure could constrain the operating margin.
Exposure to cyclicality in the automotive industry:
Despite diversification efforts, the automotive segment continues to contribute a significant portion of the group's revenue, making its performance vulnerable to the inherent cyclicality of the industry. Demand and production volumes are influenced by macroeconomic factors such as economic growth, interest rates, fuel prices, regulatory changes, and consumer sentiment. Any prolonged downturn in the automotive s
ector could adversely affect the group's revenue growth and earnings profile.
Liquidity
Strong
Bank limit utilisation was moderate at 69.63% on average for the 12 months ended March 31, 2026.
Annual
cash accrual is expected at Rs 320–410 crore against yearly term debt obligation of Rs
35–70
crore
over the medium term. Liquid investments of around Rs 336.45 crore in shares, debentures and mutual funds, along with current account balances and unencumbered fixed deposits as on March 31, 2026, supports the ove
rall liquidity profile. Low gearing and moderate networth support financial flexibility and provide cushion in case of any adverse conditions or downturn in the business.
Outlook
Stable
Crisil Ratings believes the Suprajit group shall continue to benefit from its established market position in India, diversified revenue profile and strong operating efficiency.

About the Group
SEL
was incorporated in Bengaluru in 1985 and is promoted by Ajith Kumar Rai. The company manufactures mechanical control cables for two-wheelers, pass
enger vehicles, commercial vehicles, and various non-automotive applications. It also produces automotive instrumentation products, including speedometers, tachometers, fuel and temperature gauges, and has a presence in the automotive lighting segment through the manufacture of halogen lamps. SEL is a leading player in its core businesses, serving OEMs and aftermarket customers across two-wheeler, passenger vehicle, commercial vehicle, and non-automotive segments. The company is listed on both the National
Stock Exchange (NSE) and BSE Limited.
