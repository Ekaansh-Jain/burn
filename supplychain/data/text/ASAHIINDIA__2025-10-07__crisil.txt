Moderate capex requirement
AIS operates in a capital-intensive industry where a downturn in the end-user industries may affect profitability. AIS has made significant investments, including the successful commissioning of its third float glass capacity at the Soniyana plant in March 2025, which will be benefiting through backward integration. The stabili
s
ation and ramp up of
operations leading to improved profitability will remain monitorable.
AIS has moderate capex of ~Rs 1,000 crore by end of fiscal 2027. This includes ongoing capex undertaken for cold repair of its Roorkee plant, which is expected to restart operations in
third quarter of fiscal 2026
. Furthermore, the company plans to initiate the fourth phase of its plant in Patan (Gujarat) in the current fiscal, with completion expected by the end of fiscal 2027. However, with the rising demand of glass from the construc
tion and auto sectors, the company may look out for expansion to leverage the opportunities in the future. However, higher-than-expected, debt-funded capex impacting the debt protection metrics will remain a key sensitivity factor.
Susceptibility to inherent cyclicality in the auto industry
AIS
derives 70% of its revenue from the
Auto Glass
segment, which is inherently cyclical. In the past,
auto OEMs were adversely hit by the
Covid-19
pandemic as well as slowdown in the Indian economy and recovered only f
rom the second half of fiscal 2021.
Shortage of semi-conductor chips also impacted the industry in fiscal 2023.
The
performance of AIS also remains vulnerable to economic downturns.
Liquidity
Strong
Cash and equivalent stood at Rs 174 crore as on March 31, 2025. Annual cash accrual of Rs 600-800 crore over the next two fiscals should comfortably
cover annual debt obligation and equity requirement of the capex.
The total fund-based limit of Rs 535 crore had low utilisation of ~33% during the 12 months th
rough April 2025. Unutilised bank lines should be adequate for meeting any incremental working capital requirement. AIS benefits from strong financial flexibility due to the continued support of its promoters, AGC and MSIL, which have strong credit profiles.
ESG profile
T

has moderate environmental and social impact, driven by its raw material sourcing strategies and energy-in
tensive processes. AIS has continuously focused on mitigating its environmental and social impact.
Key ESG highlights
AIS reported a high waste recycling rate (~79% of waste generated) and a relatively low proportion of water discharged (~2% of total water drawn) in fiscal 2025. It fared better than its peers on non-hazardous waste generation intensity. That said, its intensities on Scope 1 and 2 emissions and energy consumption was above the peer average.
The company reported nil lost-time injury frequency rate and fatalities of its workforce in fiscal 2025, the share of employees and workers trained on skills and safety was higher compared to its peers and there were nil stakeholders (employees and workers, value chain partners and local communities) complaints.
The gender diversity of workers was in-line with the peers at ~6%, while the attrition rate was relatively higher than its peers at ~19%.

About the Company
AIS is India's largest integrated glass solutions company and a dominant player in the auto and architectural glass segments. It commands over 75% market share in the Indian passenger car glass market. It has significant presence in the auto and architectural glass value chains through the following business verticals: auto glass, architectural glass (float, soft coat, hard coat, architectural processing) and consumer glass (auto and architectural glass).
AIS has manufacturing units in Ba
wal, Haryana; Taloja, Maharashtra; Roorkee, Uttarakhand; Chennai, Tamil Nadu; Soniyana, Rajasthan; and Patan, Gujarat; and
four
auto glass assembly units.
