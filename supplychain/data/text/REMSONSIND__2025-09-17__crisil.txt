Comfortable financial risk profile:

About the Company
RIL, incorporated in 1971, manufactures automotive non-electrical components like control cables, flexible shafts, push pull cables and gear shift assembly.
It has 4 manufacturing units in Pune (Maharashtra), Gurgaon (Haryana), Daman and Pardi (Gujarat).
RIL is promoted by Mr. Krishna Kejriwal, Mrs. Chand Kejriwal, Mr. Rahul Kejriwal and Ms. Shivani Kejriwal. It is listed on National Stock Exchange of India and Bombay Stock Exchange.
