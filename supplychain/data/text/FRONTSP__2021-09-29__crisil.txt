Despite repeated attempts to engage with the managem

CRISIL Ratings believes that rating

About the Company
FSL, incorporated in 1981, was promoted by Mr Kundal Lal Bhatia. It manufactures high-capacity springs with diameters of 13-65 millimetres (mm) that are used in heavy engineering industries. It has two plants, one each in Kanpur (Uttar Pradesh) and Poanta
Sahib (Himachal Pradesh), with a total installed capacity of 8000 tonne per annum (tpa). FSL has set up a forging unit (in fiscal 2013) in its Kanpur plant, which has a capacity of 1200 tpa.
