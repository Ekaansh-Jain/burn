Rationale
The rating action for Uno Minda Limited (UML) continues to factor in the company’s strong business profile, supported by its
established market position in the Indian automotive component industry, diversified presence across multiple product
categories, vehicle segments and customers, as well as established technology partnerships with global players. UML has
consistently expanded its product portfolio and increased content per vehicle over the years, enabling it to outperform
underlying automotive industry growth while strengthening its competitive position across traditional as well as emerging
automotive technologies. The robust business profile provides comfort regarding the company’s ability to record healthy
earnings going forward, which is expected to help the company maintain healthy credit metrics.
UML reported another year of healthy operating performance in FY2026, with consolidated revenues increasing by 17.2% YoY
to Rs. 19,658 crore. Growth remained broad based across key businesses including switches, lighting, castings, seating systems
and green mobility solutions, supported by higher wallet share with OEMs, increasing premiumisation, penetration into new
vehicle platforms and rising adoption of feature-rich products such as advanced lighting systems, airbags, sensors and ADAS-
related components. The company continued to secure sizeable order wins across product segments, including lighting,
seating, infotainment and sunroof systems, providing healthy medium-term revenue visibility. Revenue growth is expected to
remain supported by the ramp-up of recently commissioned capacities across alloy wheels, switches, lighting, airbags and
electronics businesses, with a majority of the ongoing projects expected to commence production or witness meaningful scale-
up during FY2027. In addition, UML’s growing export business, supported by increasing customer acceptance in overseas
markets, is expected to enhance revenue diversification over the medium term.

The ratings continue to derive comfort from UML’s healthy financial risk profile, characterised by strong internal accrual
generation, comfortable leverage levels and robust debt protection metrics despite elevated investment activity. Although
debt levels increased to support capacity expansions, land acquisitions and investments in subsidiaries, the company
maintained a conservative capital structure, with total debt/tangible net worth of 0.4 times and total debt/OPBDITA of 1.2
times as on March 31, 2026. Coverage indicators also remained strong, with interest coverage at around 12 times. The liquidity
profile remains comfortable, supported by healthy cash flow generation, cash and liquid investments of around Rs. 361 crore
and adequate unutilised bank lines.
UML continues to undertake sizeable investments towards capacity expansion, localisation, new product development and
future mobility technologies. The company incurred capex of around Rs. 1,560 crore in FY2026 and has guided for capex of
around Rs. 1,750 crore in FY2027. Most of these investments are linked to confirmed customer orders and capacity
augmentation across existing businesses as well as emerging technology segments. Key projects include expansion of alloy
wheel, lighting, switching and airbag capacities, establishment of a sunroof manufacturing facility under a technology licence
arrangement with AISIN Corporation, and development of EV powertrain facilities for electric drive units (EDUs) and dedicated
hybrid transmission (DHT) systems. While the elevated capex programme may keep free cash flows suppressed in the near
term, the order-backed nature of these investments and strong operating cash generation provide adequate visibility regarding
utilisation and returns over the medium term.
The company has also been acquiring land parcels for setting up new plants, anticipating healthy medium-term demand across
OEMs. These acquisitions have been funded through a mix of internal accruals and long-term borrowings at competitive rates.
While UML is subject to risks related to the acceleration of debt repayment in case of covenant breaches, its track record of
prudent financial management and strong operational performance provides comfort.
The Stable outlook reflects ICRA’s expectation that UML’s credit profile will remain healthy over the medium term, aided by
steady cash accruals on the back of its strong market position across key product categories. ICRA has withdrawn the ratings
assigned to the commercial paper programme of UML at the request of the company and based on the confirmation from the
company that there is no amount outstanding as on date, and in accordance with ICRA’s policy on withdrawal of credit ratings.
Key rating drivers and their description
Credit strengths
Diversified business profile across segments, customers and products – UML's business profile remains well diversified across
automotive components, vehicle segments and customers, supporting resilience against demand fluctuations in any single
segment. In FY2026, around 25% of revenues were derived from switches, 22% from lighting, 19% from castings, 7% from
green mobility and EV-related businesses, and 7% from seating systems, with the balance generated from products such as
airbags, sensors, infotainment systems, alloy wheels, batteries and alternate-fuel systems. Passenger vehicles accounted for
48% of revenues in FY2026, followed by two-wheelers at 42%, commercial vehicles at 4%, three-wheelers at 3% and others at
3%. The company also maintains a diversified customer base across leading domestic and global OEMs, while exports are
emerging as an additional growth driver. The diversified product mix, customer profile and end-market exposure, along with
increasing content per vehicle and expansion into EV and advanced automotive technologies, provide strong revenue visibility
and support UML's business risk profile.
Established market position in most product segments – UML is the largest automotive switch and PV alloy-wheel
manufacturer, and the second-largest player in automotive seating and lighting in the domestic market. Together, these four
product segments accounted for around 73% of the company’s consolidated revenues in FY2026. In other product segments
as well, UML enjoys a leadership position in the domestic market through its subsidiaries and JVs. The company’s strong market
position provides healthy revenue visibility going forward.
Enhanced technological capabilities and business prospects through global supplier collaborations; portfolio expanded for
transition to e-mobility – UML has focused on expanding into new product segments and improving its technological
capabilities by forming JVs and technical collaborations with foreign players. These collaborations have helped UML expand its

product portfolio and increase content-per-vehicle with OEMs. Over the years, Uno Minda has also built strong in-house
research and development (R&D) capabilities with more than 30 R&D and engineering centres globally. Its principal R&D
centre, known as Center for Research, Engineering and Advance Technologies (CREAT), works on new technologies and
enhances the existing product line.
Healthy financial risk profile characterised by a conservative capital structure and strong debt coverage indicators – Over
the years, UML has maintained a healthy financial risk profile, characterised by low leverage and strong debt coverage
indicators despite significant investments in capacity expansion, acquisitions and consolidation of group businesses. Although
debt levels increased to support elevated capex and land acquisitions, the company’s leverage metrics remained comfortable,
with total debt/OPBITDA improving to 1.2 times and net debt/OPBITDA to 1.1 times in FY2026, while gearing remained at
around 0.4 times. The financial profile continues to be supported by strong profitability, healthy operating cash flows and
prudent financial management. Going forward, debt levels are expected to remain elevated due to the planned capex of
around Rs. 1,750 crore in FY2027 and ongoing investments in growth initiatives. Nevertheless, increasing revenues, healthy
cash accruals and improving scale are expected to support the company’s credit profile, with leverage indicators likely to
remain comfortable and total debt/OPBITDA expected to remain at 1.0-1.3 times over the medium term.
Credit challenges
Susceptible to inherent cyclicality of automotive industry – As UML derives most of its revenues (90% in FY2026) from the
domestic automotive market, and its earnings remain susceptible to the inherent cyclicality of the market. Amid multiple
headwinds faced by the automotive industry over the past few years (Covid-related lockdowns, inflationary pressures, etc.),
UML’s performance mirrored underlying industry trends to an extent. However, aided by its continuous business development
initiatives, UML was able to largely outperform industry growth.
Ongoing capex plans to constrain improvement in return indicators – Over the years, UML has undertaken sizeable
investments towards capacity expansion, new product development, acquisitions and EV-related initiatives. The company
incurred capex of around Rs. 1,560 crore in FY2026 and has guided for capex of around Rs. 1,750 crore in FY2027. The planned
investments span multiple businesses, including alloy wheels, switching systems, lighting, airbags, EV powertrain products and
other growth segments. While these investments are likely to keep free cash flows negative in the near term and may require
incremental debt funding, the impact on the credit profile is expected to remain manageable, supported by healthy operating
cash flows and earnings growth. Further, a large part of the expansion is backed by customer orders and established business
relationships, which should support timely ramp-up and capacity utilisation. The company is also expected to maintain a
comfortable leverage profile, with total debt/OPBITDA likely to remain at 1.0-1.3 times over the medium term.
Environmental and social risks
Environmental considerations: Even though UML is not directly exposed to climate transition risks stemming from a likelihood
of tightening emission control requirements, with a large part of its product portfolio being used across different fuel
powertrains, its automotive manufacturing customers remain highly exposed to such risks. Accordingly, UML’s prospects
remain linked to the ability of its customers to meet tightening emission requirements. The company may need to invest
materially to develop products catering to electric vehicles, even though the transition towards these vehicles in the segments
served is likely to remain gradual.
Social considerations: UML, like most automotive component suppliers, has a significant dependence on human capital.
Hence, retaining human capital, maintaining healthy employee relations, and sustaining a robust supplier ecosystem remain
essential for disruption-free operations. Another social risk faced by UML pertains to product safety and quality, wherein
instances of product recalls and high warranty costs may not only lead to financial implications but could also harm the
company’s reputation and create a more long-lasting adverse impact.

Liquidity position: Adequate
The company’s liquidity position remains adequate, supported by expectations of healthy cash flow generation and
consolidated cash balances of Rs. 361 crore (as on March 31, 2026) and unutilised working capital limits (Rs. 566 crore as on
April 30, 2026 on a standalone basis). These resources are likely to remain more than adequate to help service the Group’s
repayment obligations and capex plans. In addition, the Group’s strong financial flexibility and proven ability to access capital
markets provide further comfort.
Rating sensitivities
Positive factors – The company’s ability to demonstrate significant improvement in the scale of operations, while maintaining
healthy return indicators and credit metrics, would be factored in favourably for a rating improvement.
Negative factors – Sustained moderation in operating performance due to weakening automotive demand, or significant debt-
funded capex, or inorganic expansion leading to deterioration in the financial risk profile, could result in a negative rating
action. Specifically, a sustained increase in leverage, with total debt /OPBDITA remaining above 1.5 times, would be a key
negative rating trigger.

About the company
Uno Minda Limited (erstwhile Minda Industries Limited; changed w.e.f. July 14, 2022), the flagship company of the Uno Minda
Group, is one of the most diversified auto component manufacturers in India with a presence across multiple product
segments, including automotive switches, lighting, acoustics, alloy wheels and die-casting, and seating systems, among others.
The company enjoys market leadership across products and is the largest supplier of switches for PVs and 2Ws as well as
automotive horns in India. Besides, it is the largest manufacturer of PV alloy wheels by capacity. Additionally, UML is the
second-largest player in automotive lighting products and a leading player in the automotive seating space for commercial
vehicles, tractors and 2Ws. It also enjoys a leading position in other product segments such as alternate fuel systems and e
2W-specific components through its subsidiaries and JVs.
Over the years, UML has scaled up substantially and diversified its business profile through acquisitions, an increase in
greenfield projects, and consolidation of Group companies in the auto component business. The company has also established
multiple JVs with global automotive majors, which have helped expand its product portfolio, strengthen its content per vehicle
with OEMs, and gain technological know-how over time.
