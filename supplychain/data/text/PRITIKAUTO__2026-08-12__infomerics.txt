Rationale
The rating assigned to Pritika Auto Industries Limited (PAIL) factors in the growing scale of
operations and comfortable capital structure and debt coverage indicators. The ratings further
derive strength from the extensive experience of promoters and established operational track
record of the group in auto component industry. Additional comfort is drawn from the company’s
strong relationships with OEMs and repeat business from reputed customers
However, these rating strengths are partially offset by the moderation in profitability margin,
high working capital-intensive nature of operations, exposure to inherent cyclicality in auto
industry along with high client concentration risk.
Outlook: Stable
The ‘Stable’ outlook reflects Infomerics’ expectation of healthy growth in scale and improvement
in profitability over the medium term while maintaining adequate debt protection metrics.

The total operating income of the group grew at a CAGR of 10.08% during last three years
ending March 31, 2026, further on y-o-y the TOI grew by 35.32% and stood at Rs. 482.95
crore in FY26 as against Rs. 356.88 crore in FY25 on account of increase in sales of its key
products. Despite increase in scale of operations, the EBITDA margin declined by 130 bps
and stood at 14.71% in FY26 as compared to 16.01% in FY25 primarily on account of
increase in power and fuel cost and workshop expenses along with increase in raw
material consumption cost. Subsequently, the PAT Margin also declined by 174 bps and
stood at 4.78% in FY26 from 6.52% in FY25 on account of decline in operational
profitability and lower other income.
• Comfortable capital structure and debt coverage indicators
The capital structure of the group remained comfortable as evidenced by overall gearing
ratio stood at 0.79x as on March 31, 2026, compared to 0.74x as on March 31, 2025, on
account of increase in term loan along with higher utilization of working capital bank
limits as on balance sheet date by the group. The long-term debt to equity ratio stood at
0.46x as on March 31, 2026, compared to 0.41x as on March 31, 2025. The total
indebtedness of the group stood moderate as reflected by TOL/TNW of 1.23x as on March
31, 2026, compared to 1.08x as on March 31, 2025, on account of increase in creditors
along with increase in bank borrowings. The debt coverage indicators stood comfortable
marked by interest coverage ratio of the group at 3.21x in FY26 deteriorated from 3.57x
in FY25 on account of increase in interest and finance charges. DSCR stood at 1.65x in
FY26 as against 1.87x as on 31st Mar’25 on account of owing to higher debt servicing
obligations relative to the gross cash accruals generated during the year.
• Extensive experience of promoters and established operational track record of the
Group in auto component industry
The group was promoted by Late Mr. Raminder Singh Nibber, a mechanical engineer with
over three decades of experience in manufacturing tractor and automobile components
through its flagship company, Pritika Industries Limited (PIL), established in 1974. His
son, Mr. Harpreet Singh Nibber, the current Managing Director, has more than two
decades of experience and has been instrumental in driving the group’s growth and
diversification. Over the years, the group has expanded its operations by setting up Pritika
Engineering Components Limited (PECL) in 2018 and Meeta Castings Limited (MCL) in
FY23, equipped with advanced technology for manufacturing key tractor components
such as axle housings and crank case cylinder blocks. Supported by a qualified
management team and long-standing relationships with reputed OEMs, the group has

• Strong relationships with OEMs and repeat business from reputed customers
The group has developed strong and long-standing relationships with leading OEMs in
the tractor and automobile industry, resulting in a stable and recurring order base. Over
the years, it has established itself as a trusted supplier to reputed OEMs such as Mahindra
& Mahindra, Tractor and Farm Equipment Ltd (TAFE), Swaraj Engines Limited, Escorts
Limited, and CNH Industrial Private Limited, among others. The long-term association
with multiple OEMs not only ensures revenue visibility but also reflects the group’s
credibility, technical capability, and ability to meet evolving industry standards.
Weaknesses
• Working capital intensive nature of operations
The group’s operating cycle stood elongated and stood at 142 days in FY26, improved
from 178 days in FY25 due to the decline in inventory days from 162 days in FY25 to 136
days in FY26 and collection periods to 28 days in in FY26 as against 42 days in FY25. The
higher inventory level is because the company has to keep enough raw material, work-in-
progress, and finished goods for different OEM requirements and specifications. The
multi-stage production and quality inspection processes also add to work-in-progress
inventory, resulting in elevated working capital requirements despite a moderate
collection period of around 28 days in FY26.
• Exposed to inherent cyclicality in auto industry
The group operates in the automotive sector, which is inherently cyclical and closely
linked to overall economic conditions. The demand from automotive OEMs tends to
fluctuate with changes in consumer sentiment and macroeconomic trends.
• Exposure to high client concentration risks
The group continues to have a concentrated customer profile, with a major portion of its
revenues derived from a few large OEMs. Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd. and Escorts Kubota
Ltd. remain the key customers, contributing approximately 47% of the company’s total
revenues. Such dependence on a limited customer base exposes the company to business

Liquidity – Adequate
The liquidity position of the group is expected to remain adequate as it expects sufficient cushion
in its cash accruals vis-à-vis debt repayments. The consolidated average fund-based utilisation
for last 12 months ending June 2026 stood at 78.73%, indicating sufficient cushion cover
availability in case of additional working capital requirement. The unencumbered cash and bank
balance of the group stood at Rs. 6.10 Crore as on March 31, 2026
Rating Sensitivities
Upward Factors
- Substantial and sustained growth in operating income, profitability margins and
cash accruals.
- Total debt/ EBITDA below 1.5x on a sustained basis.
- Improvement in operating cycle
Downward Factors
- Significant decline in operating income and profitability
- Further elongation in operating cycle driven by piling up of inventory or stretched
receivables.
- Any sizeable debt funded capital expenditure weakening the financial risk profile,
specific credit metric for downgrade includes DSCR below 1.50 times on sustained
basis.
About the Group
Pritika Group of Industries, established in 1974, is a diversified manufacturer of precision
machined castings and engineering components, primarily catering to the tractor and automotive
sectors. The Group has five entities with manufacturing facilities across North India and
capabilities spanning iron casting, machining and sub-assembly. Its product portfolio includes
rear axle housings, differential cases, hydraulic lift covers, wheel hubs and other critical
components. The Group has established relationships with leading OEMs including Escorts
Kubota, TAFE, Mahindra & Mahindra and Ashok Leyland.

About the Company
Pritika Auto Industries Limited (PAIL), incorporated in 1980, is engaged in the manufacturing of
precision-machined castings and automobile components, primarily catering to the tractor and
commercial vehicle segments. The company manufactures products such as axle housings, wheel
housings, hydraulic lift housings, differential carriers, brake housings, cylinder blocks and
crankcases. PAIL has established relationships with leading OEMs, including Mahindra &
Mahindra, TAFE, Escorts Kubota, SML Isuzu and Ashok Leyland. The company operates
integrated foundry and machining facilities in Punjab and Himachal Pradesh, with capabilities in
grey iron and ductile iron castings and machining.
