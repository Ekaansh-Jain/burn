Detailed Rationale
CRISIL Ratings has revised its outlook on the long-term bank facilities of ASK Automotive Ltd (AAL; part of the ASK group) to
'Positive'
from ‘Stable’ while reaffirmed the rating at
'CRISIL AA-’
, while reaffirmed its ‘CRISIL A1+' rating on the short-term bank loan facility.
The
outlook
revision
reflects CRISIL belief that the business risk profile of the group will remain benefited from the capacity expansion undertaken by the management in
the company’s
wholly owned subsidiary, ASK Automobiles Private Limi
ted (AAPL), by improving the proximity to the customer, hence leading to improved volumes and better efficiencies. The revision in outlook also factors in the sustained improvement in the revenue of the ASK group to around Rs. 3,005 crore in fiscal 2024 from Rs 2,560 crore in fiscal 2023, driven by improved realisation and volume growth. Penetration into other segments such as electric two-wheelers, four-wheelers, and overseas markets and non-automotive segment, backed by sustained increase in demand for tw
o-wheelers, supported the revenue growth. Moreover, operating efficiency improved too, as indicated by improvement in the operating margin to ~10.35% in fiscal 2024 from 9.65% in fiscal 2022, along with steady return on capital employed (ROCE). With ongoing capex in Bhiwadi and Bangalore, group’s proximity with its key customers will further increase, which will aid the business risk profile further. Added capacities in new geographies will also expand the geographical outreach of the group, further enhanci
ng the business risk profile.
The rating also factors in company’s strong financial risk profile, driven by sizeable networth and moderate reliance on external debt. While the debt requirement has consistently increased over the past 2-3 fiscals through fiscal 2024, for increased working capital and capital expenditure requirements, strong networth and pre-payment of some loans have resulted in overall gearing ratio at 0.5 time. Also, a steady increase in operating profitability has resulted in continuati
on of healthy debt protection indicators, which shall continue over the medium term as well. Liquidity, on the other hand, remains healthy too, backed by sizeable net cash accruals against maturing debt, cushion in bank lines and excess reserves available to encounter exigencies (if any).
The ratings continue to reflect the strong market position of the ASK group in the two-wheeler components industry and its robust financial risk profile. These strengths are partly offset by exposure to project related ri
sks, and limited track record of sustained improvement in operating profitability.
Analytical Approach
CRISIL Ratings has combined the business and financial risk profiles of AAL and its wholly owned subsidiary, ASK Automobiles Pvt Ltd (AAPL). This is because both the companies have operational linkages.

Key Rating Drivers & Detailed Description
Strengths:
Strong market position in the two-wheeler components industry:
The ASK group is a prominent player in the two-wheeler friction materials segment and caters to most of the renowned OEMs in India, including Hero MotoCorp Ltd (Hero MotoCorp; ‘CRISIL AAA/Stable/CRISIL A1+’), Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt Ltd (HMSI), Yamaha Motors India, TVS Motors Co Ltd, Suzuki Motorcycle India Pvt Ltd, and Bajaj Auto Ltd (Bajaj; ‘CRISIL AAA/Stable/CRISIL A1+’). Presence in the overseas markets and aftermarket segment
, and diversified product mix including brake shoes, aluminium die castings, panel assemblies, support the market position. Consequently, the group has been able to achieve healthy revenue performance despite the moderate industry performance over the past few fiscals through fiscal 2024. The group’s revenue rose 17% on-year to over Rs 3,000 crore in fiscal 2024 and is estimated to have grown at a compound annual rate (CAGR) of 18% over the past four fiscals through fiscal 2024. The revenue is expected to i
ncrease further, with addition of units in Bhiwadi and Bengaluru, and the management’s focus on diversifying into the passenger vehicle segment and increasing exports and penetration in the electric two-wheeler space. Ramp-up of operations in new units leading to sustained improvement in the business risk profile remains monitorable.
Robust financial risk profile:
The financial risk profile has improved consistently because of steady repayment of debt backed by sufficient reserve, and reduced reliance on bank lines on account of prudent working capital management. The ASK group is undertaking a large, debt-funded capex (of Rs 400-450 crore), spanning over fiscal 2024 and 2025, to set up units through its wholly owned subsidiary, ASK Automobiles Private Limited. Despite the stated capex, the capital structure should remain robust support
ed by a sizeable networth. The total outside liabilities to adjusted networth ratio was 0.90-1.00 times as on March 31, 2024, and is expected at 0.9-1.0 times over the medium term. Debt protection metrics have been robust and will remain so over the medium term, backed by steady operating profitability. Interest coverage and net cash accrual to total debt ratios were at 10-11 times and 0.7-0.8 time, respectively, for fiscal 2024.
Weaknesses:
Exposure to project related risks:
AAPL (ASK Automobiles Private Limited) wholly owned subsidiary, is undertaking capital expenditure of Rs. 400-450 crore spanning over fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2025 which will be funded via debt to the tune of Rs. 220-250 crore. Given the management expertise and know-how in the industry, CRISIL believes that the management will be able to implement it in a timely manner, however, its stabilization thereafter will remain imperative. The project is expected to be completed by
Jan’25. Any time or cost overrun can further impact the credit risk profile of the group and will remain monitorable too.
Limited track record of sustained improvement in operating profitability:
The operating profitability has remained volatile over the years, ranging from 10% to 12% over the four years through fiscal 2024. While the group remains insulated from input price volatility, as the same is passed on by respective OEMs, significant increase in overheads and/or delay in pass on of price hike by OEMs and limited pass on in the aftermarket has impacted profitability. While operating profitability improved in fiscal 202
4 to ~10.35% from 9.65% in the previous fiscal, track record of sustained improvement in the same remains limited. CRISIL Ratings believes that the operating profitability of the ASK group is likely to improve with the setting up of new units, due to increased proximity resulting in savings on logistics cost, better cost absorption with ramp up of operations in new units, however, its sustainability will remain a key monitorable.
Liquidity
:
Strong
Bank limit utilisation was low at 41.59% on average for the 12 months through March 2024. Cash accrual is expected at Rs 270-280 crore against term debt obligation of Rs. 70-75 crore over the medium term, and will cushion liquidity. Current ratio was moderate at 1.07 times on March 31, 2023. Low gearing and moderate networth provide financial flexibility to withstand adverse conditions or downturn in the business.
Outlook
:
Positive
CRISIL believes that the business risk profile will further improve amidst setting up of new units and expected improvement in proximity to customers, though timely completion of capex and its ramp up will remain a key monitorable.
Rating Sensitivity factors
Upward factors:
Timely completion of capex with no cost overrun leading to healthy financial risk profile.
Sustained and significant increase in sales volume and stable operating margin of 10-11%
Downward factors:
Steep decline in revenue or profitability resulting in annual net cash accrual less than Rs 100 crore.
Stretch in the working capital cycle or sizeable, debt-funded capex adversely impacting the capital structure and hence financial risk profile.
About the Group
Incorporated in 1988 and based in Manesar, Haryana, AAL manufactures components used in advanced braking systems, aluminium lightweighting precision solutions and safety control cables, such as brake shoes, safety control cables and other aluminium pressure die casting (PDC) and machined components, such as brake panels, crank cases, and hubs. Mr K S Rathee and his wife, Ms Vijay Rathee, are the promoters. Their children, Mr Prashant Rathee and Mr Aman Rathee, have also joined the business a
nd are part of the board.
AAPL is a wholly owned subsidiary of AAL and was incorporated in June 2021. It has recently set up manufacturing plants at Bhiwadi in Rajasthan and in Bengaluru for manufacturing auto components and parts. The new facilities became operational in April 2024
